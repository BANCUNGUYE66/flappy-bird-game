# Retro Flappy Bird Game

A classic Flappy Bird game built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript featuring retro pixel-art styling.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click the **START GAME** button to begin
3. Keep the bird flying by:
   - Pressing the **SPACEBAR** key, OR
   - **Clicking** anywhere on the game canvas
4. Navigate through the green pipes without hitting them
5. Avoid hitting the ground
6. Try to score as many points as possible!

## Game Features

- **Retro pixel-art graphics** with bold outlines
- **Simple one-button controls** (Space or Click)
- **Score tracking system**
- **Smooth animations** with canvas rendering
- **Collision detection** for pipes and ground
- **Game over screen** with final score display
- **Restart functionality**

## Game Controls

- **SPACE** or **CLICK**: Make the bird flap and fly upward
- The bird will fall due to gravity when you don't press anything

## Tips

- Time your flaps carefully
- Don't tap too rapidly or the bird will fly too high
- Find a rhythm to navigate through the pipes
- Each pipe you pass earns you 1 point

Enjoy the game!
